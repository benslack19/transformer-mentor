Q2: Diminishing Returns Assessment

📈 Big jump between B1 and B2: A win rate rose ~8% from batch 1 (Jun 17) to batch 2 (Jun 21).

📉 Plateau thereafter: Batches 2–8 fluctuate within ±1.2% std — no statistically significant monotonic trend (Mann-Kendall p = 0.11).

⚠️ Batch sizes vary: B1 has only 97 tasks vs. B7's 969 — early batches carry wider uncertainty.

⚠️ Category composition shifts: "Other" and "Websites & Apps" are absent from 4 early batches — could partially explain the B1→B2 jump.

Interpretation: The model improved significantly early in the evaluation window but appears to have plateaued. Real-world signal will now be more informative than continued lab evaluation.

Q5: Recommendation

✅ Launch (with monitoring)
  • A beats B on 54% of tasks vs 26% — robust margin
  • Wilcoxon p = 4.9e-69 — statistically significant
  • Win rate has plateaued — diminishing returns from continued lab iteration
  • Real-world traffic will yield higher-signal feedback faster
⚠️ Conditions & Caveats
  • Review Model A's most common error types before ship
  • Staged rollout recommended (e.g., 10% traffic first)
  • Monitor production win rate weekly — pause if <50% at 2σ
  • Improve future evals: random grader assignment vs. self-selection
